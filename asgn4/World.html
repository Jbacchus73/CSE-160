GRADER NOTES

  • Spotlight: The glowing sun is the spotlight — toggle with the Spotlight (Sun) buttons. I added a simple day/night cycle for creativity.
  • Point Light: The yellow cube marks it. Moves over time and via the Light X/Y/Z sliders. Dragging a slider pauses auto-motion; Light Anim ON resumes it.
  • Light Color: The color swatch changes the point light color.
  • Normals: The Normal Visualization buttons show normals as color.
  • OBJ Model: The low-poly trees are loaded from an OBJ file.
